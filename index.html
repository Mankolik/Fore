How to play
  • Aim: drag anywhere on the course (or use ⟲ ⟳ for fine tuning).
  • Shot: pick a shot type from the row of buttons: Full, ¾ (controlled), Chip (low bump & run, metered by total distance incl. roll), Flop (high & soft, over bunkers), Punch (low, under trees) or Putt.
  • Lie & risk: long clubs from sand or deep rough are likely to be chunked, thinned or topped — the ⚠ % under the club shows the risk. A well-timed swing cuts it; 56°/60° wedges are built for bunkers and hybrids for the rough.
  • Around the green: chips are the safe play when you can land the ball on the green (their pin mark reads the grass and the up/downhill of your line, not the break). Flops stop quickly over bunkers but are a big swing and easy to blade off tight lies.
  • Bunkers: greenside and pot bunkers have a steep lip. Low shots from close to the face catch it and drop back in; a Flop with the 56°/60° (the open-faced splash) is the reliable way out, and the safest.
  • Club: ‹ › steps through the clubs that can play that shot — 15 in the bag incl. hybrids and 52°/56°/60° wedges. A shot and club are suggested each time.
  • Swing: hold the button for the backswing, release to set power.
  • Accuracy: tap again as the marker returns to the white line. Early = slice right, late = hook left.
  • The pin mark on the meter shows the power needed to carry the flag (wind & slopes not included).
  • Long game: even pure strikes wander a little (more with the driver than a wedge). Wind is shown as a gust range — it varies shot to shot — and isn't in the pin mark, so aim off and club up or down. Watch for flyer lies, balls sitting down, divots and plugged lies: they're announced but not in the pin mark either.
  • Worlds: each seed is set in a world — parkland, desert, alien (low gravity!), coastal links, winter (slippery frozen ponds) or volcanic (lava). The preview above shows which; tap 🎲 for another.
  • Trees: now and then a lone tree stands in the fairway. If one blocks your line, a Punch is suggested: it stays low enough to run under the branches (not through the trunk!).
  • Wind, slopes, lie, trees, bunkers and water all matter. Arrows on the green show the slope when putting.
  • Hold the button during a shot to fast-forward.
  • Lip-outs: a putt that catches the hole only drops if it isn't rolling too fast. Dead-centre putts can be firm (they rattle in off the back); one clipping the edge has to be dying. Too firm and it lips out — or races straight over.
  • Your rounds: 🏆 on the main menu lists your best and worst finished rounds with the course, seed and scorecard — replay any of them.
  • Menu (☰): scorecard, sound effects, music on/off, next track and the main menu. Tap the minimap for an overview of the hole.
  • Desktop: mouse to aim, click/space to swing, ←/→ fine aim, ↑/↓ clubs, S next shot type, N next music track.
